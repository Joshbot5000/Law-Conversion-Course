# Career and commercial awareness

This folder is the "proof of work" side of the repository: short, clear explainers showing that you can take a legal or commercial development and explain **why it matters to clients and law firms**.

## Why it helps applications
A training contract application or interview can ask about a recent deal or development. Each explainer here is something you can point to (or link from LinkedIn) as evidence that you research, write clearly and understand technology, rather than simply claiming it.

## How to add one
1. Copy [`commercial-awareness/TEMPLATE.md`](commercial-awareness/TEMPLATE.md).
2. Name it with the date and topic, e.g. `2026-10-ai-copyright-ruling.md`.
3. Fill it in in your own words and commit.

Your weekly LinkedIn post ideas are a natural source: when one becomes a post, turn it into an explainer here too.

> **Before making this repository public:** only publish material written in your own words. Don't upload University of Law course materials, slides or textbook extracts; those belong to their authors.
