# Problem-question skeleton: contract (formation → breach → remedies)

## Part A — Was a contract formed?
1. **Offer or invitation to treat?** (*Fisher v Bell*, *Boots*, *Carlill*)
2. **Still open?** Revocation communicated (*Byrne*); counter-offer (*Hyde v Wrench*) vs request for information (*Stevenson v McLean*).
3. **Acceptance:** mirror image; communicated; silence (*Felthouse*); postal rule (*Adams v Lindsell*); instantaneous (*Entores*); battle of the forms (*Butler Machine Tool*).
4. **Intention to create legal relations:** commercial presumed; domestic presumed not (*Balfour*), rebuttable (*Merritt*).
5. **Consideration** (Chapter 2).

## Part B — What are the terms and were they broken?
- Express and implied terms; classification (condition / warranty / innominate term); exclusion clauses (Chapter 3).

## Part C — Remedies
- **Expectation loss:** put C in the position as if the contract had been performed (*Robinson v Harman* (1848)).
- **Remoteness:** losses arising naturally, or in the reasonable contemplation of both parties at the time of contracting (*Hadley v Baxendale* (1854)).
- **Mitigation:** C cannot recover losses they could reasonably have avoided (*British Westinghouse* [1912]).
